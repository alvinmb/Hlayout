µA741

General-purpose internally-compensated bipolar op-amp, supplied as an unpackaged die for hybrid and chip-and-wire assembly. Seven aluminium bond pads in standard 8-pin order.

Assembly notes

  • Substrate is live. The die backside sits at VCC−. Mount on a VCC− island, or on an isolated pad kept clear of positive-rail copper.
  • Offset trim. Wire a 10 kΩ pot across pads 1 and 5 with the wiper to VCC−; omit if the application tolerates the untrimmed offset.
  • Bond order. Pads follow the 8-pin DIP sequence — 1→4 down the left edge, 7→5 down the right — which keeps bond wires short and non-crossing on a standard footprint.
  • Handling. Bare die has no package protection: work under ESD control, in a controlled-atmosphere or nitrogen environment, and glob-top or lid the cavity after bonding.

Modelling in HLayout

Build a device cell for the die — a ~1.1 mm body outline plus seven pads on the land layer, each tagged pin="1"…pin="7" at the edge coordinates above — then place it like any attached part and route to the pads.

c = lib.new_cell("UA741_DIE")
c.add_path(OUTLINE, square(1100), width=20)          # 1.1 mm die edge
c.add_rectangle(LAND, -640, 380, -540, 480, pin="1") # left column, pin 1
# ... pads 2,3,4 down -x ; pads 7,6,5 down +x
s.place_cell("UA741_DIE", (x, y))
